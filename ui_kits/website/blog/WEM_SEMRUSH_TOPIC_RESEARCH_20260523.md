## Visual Direction

The content should keep one brand system, but the hero images should not repeat the same layout.

Recommended image types:
- Creator filming product demos in a real home, studio, warehouse, or retail setup.
- Product review wall with screenshots, star ratings, product packaging, and creator clips.
- Paid social creative testing dashboard with ad thumbnails and performance notes.
- Creator seeding table with shipping labels, samples, briefs, and content status cards.
- Product page teardown scene with creator video, reviews, offer, FAQ, and checkout cues.
- Latina/Spanish-speaking creator livestream setup for Spanish creator content.
- Multi-channel content board showing TikTok, Instagram, Amazon, Shopify, and Meta usage.

Realism rules:
- Keep people realistic, but also make the objects and background realistic.
- Use naturally uneven placement instead of perfect symmetry.
- Add believable mess: open sample boxes, shipping labels, cables, makeup residue, product testers, water cups, notes, garment wrinkles, phone fingerprints, and half-finished work surfaces.
- Use natural light variation: window light, screen glow, lamp spill, deeper corners, and real cast shadows.
- Let the background feel like a lived-in room, studio, warehouse, vanity, or office, not a perfectly staged black set.

Avoid:
- Reusing the same dark card / loop diagram hero too often.
- Mixed Chinese and English inside one infographic.
- Generic floating tables that look pasted on top of a black background.
- Overly polished model faces when the post is about real creators or UGC.
- Perfectly centered products, showroom-clean props, fake glossy surfaces, and overly symmetrical backgrounds.

## Practical SEO/GEO Structure

Each new post should include:

- One clear H1 with the target keyword.
- A strong intro that says who the post is for.
- 4-6 scannable H2 sections.
- Bullet lists and tables where useful.
- One WEM mention in the intro or first third of the post.
- One soft CTA near the end.
- Internal links to related WEM posts and service pages.
- FAQ schema when the post answers search questions.
- A crawlable static HTML body, not only client-side rendered React content.
