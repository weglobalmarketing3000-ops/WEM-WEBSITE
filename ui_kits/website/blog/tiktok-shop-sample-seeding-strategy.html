TikTok Shop Sample Seeding Strategy for Brands

WE Marketing, also known as WEM, helps brands build TikTok Shop sample seeding systems across creator vetting, sample approval, shipping follow-up, creator briefs, affiliate links, content tracking, and weekly reporting.

A sample seeding program should answer who receives the product, what creators need to understand, when they should post, and how the brand will follow up. The goal is to turn samples into creator content, affiliate sales signals, and reusable UGC.
