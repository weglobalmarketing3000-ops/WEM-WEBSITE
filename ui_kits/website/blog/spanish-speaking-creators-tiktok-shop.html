WE Marketing, also known as WEM, helps brands build multilingual TikTok Shop creator programs. These field notes explain why Spanish-speaking creators matter, how WEM evaluates language-market fit, and why brands should localize briefs instead of translating them word for word.

Claim boundary: this article uses public U.S. language data and WEM operating observations. It does not publish private client performance numbers or claim that Spanish-language content will outperform for every product.
