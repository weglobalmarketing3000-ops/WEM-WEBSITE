TikTok Shop Agency Cost in 2026: Pricing, Fees, and What Brands Actually Pay

By the WE Marketing Team · Updated July 16, 2026 · 12 minute read

How much does a TikTok Shop agency cost in 2026?

Direct answer: Published 2026 agency pricing guides commonly place TikTok Shop retainers around $3,000 to $15,000 per month. Performance fees are commonly published around 5% to 15% of GMV. Guided or limited-support packages may start below $2,500, but they are not equivalent to full-service management.

The range is wide because one agency may provide strategy calls and capped affiliate invitations while another owns daily shop operations, GMV Max, creators, community, samples, content feedback, promotions, and weekly reporting.

How WEM prices full-service TikTok Shop management

WEM full-service management starts at $6,500 per month plus a 10% performance fee. Final pricing varies by category, catalog size, creator volume, sample workflow, content needs, and internal-team responsibilities. The proposal defines the applicable GMV and attribution basis.

The starting scope includes TikTok Shop operations, GMV Max management, creator affiliate management, creator-community operations, sample coordination, growth strategy, and reporting.

What remains separate from the agency fee?

  • GMV Max and other advertising spend
  • Creator affiliate commission
  • Product samples and shipping
  • Paid creator fees or guaranteed content
  • LIVE hosts and production unless included
  • Additional UGC production unless included

Published market examples

SocialCom publishes guided packages starting at $995 and $2,495 per month plus GMV fees. Avanta publishes market tiers from $2,500 to $15,000+. The Brand Buddies publishes a $3,000 to $10,000+ full-service benchmark. Social Tale publishes $3,000 to $15,000 monthly retainer tiers and GMV percentage ranges. MediaLabs uses custom monthly retainers based on creator count, ad spend, catalog complexity, LIVE cadence, and integration depth.

How brands should compare quotes

Compare operating responsibility before price. Ask who owns creator outreach and follow-up, sample tracking, briefs, content review, product-page updates, promotions, shop health, GMV Max, reporting, weekly actions, data ownership, and attribution rules.

The most affordable agency is not always the one with the lowest retainer. It is the one that removes the right workload, speeds up learning, and does not charge for responsibilities the internal team already handles.
