About WE Marketing · WEM

A Southern California TikTok Shop agency for creator-led commerce.

WE Marketing (WEM) helps cross-border brands, Amazon sellers, DTC teams, and Chinese-speaking founders grow in the U.S. market through TikTok Shop operations, creator affiliates, UGC content, live selling, and product-page conversion work.

TikTok Shop credentials

WE Marketing is listed in TikTok Shop’s U.S. partner directory. WeMarketing is the certificate styling; WE Marketing is the public company name. These credentials confirm directory listing and training completion; they are not a promise of campaign results.

WEM is strongest for brands that need a bilingual operating partner, not only a campaign vendor: teams entering TikTok Shop US, Chinese brands localizing for American shoppers, Amazon sellers adding creator commerce, and U.S. brands that need creator outreach, sample tracking, and shop execution under one roof.
