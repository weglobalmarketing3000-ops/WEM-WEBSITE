A 60–90 day operating plan

From “shop is open” to real market signals.

A cold start is not one setup task or one creator post. Each stage should reduce a different risk before the brand spends more.

01 · READINESS

Choose the hero SKU and remove launch blockers

Review product eligibility, U.S. inventory and fulfillment, margin room, pricing, claims, product-page structure, affiliate settings, sample capacity, and the decision process inside the brand.

02 · CREATOR ACTIVATION

Build the first affiliate and sample wave

Set commissions, source category-fit creators, approve samples, track delivery, share usable briefs, follow up, and create enough opportunities to learn which creator profiles respond and post.

03 · TESTING

Test content, offers, and creator fit together

Compare hooks, demonstrations, routines, objections, price framing, bundles, commissions, creator types, and content formats. The goal is repeatable evidence, not a lucky view spike.

04 · SCALE DECISION

Turn early signals into the next operating plan

Review creator response, delivery-to-post rate, content output, product clicks, orders, conversion, GMV, reviews, shop health, and blockers. Scale the combinations that work; repair or stop the ones that do not.
