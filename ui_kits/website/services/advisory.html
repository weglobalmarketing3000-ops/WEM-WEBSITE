Consulting FAQ

Who is a one-time TikTok Shop strategy session for?

A one-time strategy session is for brands that are not sure what they need yet, need one focused diagnosis, or want an experienced operator to set priorities and a practical plan before the team executes.

How is Monthly Advisory different from a one-time strategy session?

Monthly Advisory is for internal teams that execute themselves and need a regular strategy review, decision support, and priority reset as the shop and creator program develop.

What is Team Enablement and Advisory?

Team Enablement and Advisory is for strong marketing or ecommerce teams that need to build TikTok Shop capability. Cadence can be weekly or biweekly based on scope, with working sessions across fundamentals, shop setup, Affiliate Center, creator workflows, commission logic, content, offers, reporting, and operating decisions.

What if we need hands-on execution?

Full-Service Management is separately scoped for brands that need WE Marketing to operate the work alongside their team.
