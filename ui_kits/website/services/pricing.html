Four common pricing models

Match the commercial model to the job.

The cheapest quote is not necessarily the most affordable. First decide what result and operating responsibility you are buying.

Strategy / advisory

For teams that will execute in-house

A one-time audit, launch roadmap, team workshop, or ongoing advisory relationship. The brand owns daily execution.

Cold-start project

For brands launching from zero

A defined first phase covering readiness, affiliate setup, initial creator seeding, content tests, early signals, and a scale decision.

Monthly retainer + performance

For ongoing creator or shop operations

A stable operating fee supports the team and cadence; a performance component aligns part of compensation with attributable commercial results.

Package pricing

For UGC and defined deliverables

Best when the brand needs a known content quantity, creator tier, editing scope, usage rights, and delivery timeline rather than full shop management.

What “affordable” should mean

Do not pay an agency to duplicate your internal team.

If your team already owns shop operations, WEM can focus on creator affiliates. If you only need content, use a UGC package. If you need decisions but can execute, start with consulting. Full service makes sense when the brand wants one team accountable across the system.

Creator responsibility: Who sources, contacts, follows up, briefs, and reviews creators?
Sample responsibility: Who approves, tracks, reminds, and measures delivery-to-post?
Shop responsibility: Who updates product pages, promotions, affiliate settings, and shop health?
Content responsibility: Who gives feedback and turns performance into the next brief?
Reporting responsibility: Does the report name blockers, owners, and next actions—or only show numbers?
What changes the price

Scope drivers a useful proposal should explain.

Category & compliance

Claims, eligibility, education needs, and risk vary across beauty, skincare, health, wellness, fashion, and other categories.

Priority SKUs

One hero product requires a different launch system than a broad catalog with multiple offers and inventory positions.

Creator volume

Outreach, samples, briefs, reminders, content review, and relationship management scale with creator activity.

Content & live needs

UGC production, editing, paid usage, live hosts, moderation, and campaign support add distinct workstreams.

Shop readiness

A healthy shop with clear product pages needs less repair than a new or underperforming shop with unresolved blockers.

Internal capacity

The more your team owns reliably, the more narrowly WEM can scope its role and reduce duplicated cost.

What is an affordable TikTok Shop agency for a growing brand?

An affordable agency matches scope to the brand's real bottleneck. A growing brand may not need full-service management. WEM can scope consulting, cold-start support, creator affiliate management, UGC packages, or full-service operations separately.

Does WEM charge a retainer or a percentage of sales?

The model depends on the engagement. Full-service management, cold-start support, and creator affiliate management commonly use a monthly retainer plus a performance component. Consulting can be project-based or ongoing, and UGC can use package pricing.

Why does WEM not publish one universal management fee?

Because a universal number hides the work. A one-SKU launch, an established brand outsourcing only creators, and a multi-SKU full-service program require different staffing, creator volume, samples, content, reporting, and accountability. WEM defines the responsibilities before quoting.

How should we compare TikTok Shop agency quotes?

Compare responsibility before price. Ask who owns creator outreach and follow-up, sample tracking, briefs, content review, product-page updates, promotions, live support, shop health, reporting, and weekly next actions. A lower fee can cost more if most execution remains with your team.

Can WEM manage only TikTok Shop creator affiliates?

Yes. If your team already runs the shop, WEM can focus on creator sourcing, Affiliate Center activity, outreach, commission planning, samples, briefs, posting follow-up, content review, and weekly affiliate reporting.
