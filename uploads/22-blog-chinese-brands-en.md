### H2: 5 things US TikTok Shop is not

```
Before talking about strategy, it is worth being explicit 
about what does not transfer.

1. The US is not a single market. The US TikTok Shop buyer 
   base is bilingual at scale (English, Spanish, and a 
   growing Chinese-speaking segment). Single-language 
   launches consistently underperform.

2. US compliance is stricter than many Chinese brands expect. 
   FDA-regulated categories (supplements, beauty, food) have 
   ingredient and claim rules. TikTok Shop's compliance 
   filter aggressively pulls videos that violate them. A 
   single banned-claim word ("treats," "cures," "prevents") 
   can pull a creator video.

3. US creators do not work like Chinese KOLs. They are 
   independent operators, not agency-managed talent in the 
   Chinese sense. Briefs are lighter, scripts are not 
   accepted, and creators expect commission plus optional 
   flat fees, not bulk-buy talent rates.

4. Live commerce is creator-led, not host-led. The Douyin 
   model of professional hosts running brand-owned long-form 
   live exists in the US but is not the dominant pattern. 
   Affiliate creators running their own short-form lives 
   drive a meaningful share of GMV.

5. Fulfillment expectations are different. US buyers expect 
   3-5 day shipping in 2026. Direct-from-China fulfillment 
   without a US warehouse hits delivery times that hurt 
   re-purchase rates.
```

### H2: 6-step entry framework

```
Step 1: US legal entity and TikTok Shop seller account
Most cross-border launches use a US LLC for liability and tax 
treatment. The TikTok Shop seller account is set up under 
that entity. This typically takes 4-8 weeks including EIN, 
bank account, and seller verification.

Step 2: US fulfillment infrastructure
Three options: 3PL warehouse on the US West Coast, Fulfilled 
by TikTok if eligible, or a hybrid with a US-based forwarder. 
Direct-from-China shipping for TikTok Shop SKUs is workable 
for high-AOV products but rarely competitive on AOV under 
$30.

Step 3: Compliance pre-launch
Pull every product claim through US FDA-compliant rewriting. 
Build a banned-claim list for creator briefs. Test the 
listing copy through TikTok Shop Academy's policy guidance. 
Skipping this step costs more later in violations and pulled 
videos.

Step 4: Multilingual creator network
EN + ES at minimum for US programs. EN + ES + 中文 unlocks 
the broader audience and lets the brand speak to first-gen 
Chinese-American buyers who are a real and underserved 
segment for many cross-border brands.

Step 5: 90-day launch with a US-based TSP
A US-based TSP partner provides the creator network, live 
infrastructure, compliance ops, and TikTok account-manager 
relationships. Cross-border brands operating without a US 
partner typically spend 60-90 days longer in cold start.

Step 6: Multi-channel expansion
Once TikTok Shop is stable, brands typically expand to 
Amazon US and DTC. The TikTok Shop creator content also 
doubles as performance creative for Meta and Google ads on 
the DTC side.
```

### H2: How to choose a US-based TSP for a Chinese brand

```
Three things matter beyond standard TSP evaluation:

1. Chinese-speaking team
Communication in Mandarin is faster and more accurate for 
ops alignment, especially in the launch phase. Look for a 
TSP with at least one fluent Mandarin operator, not just a 
translator.

2. Cross-border experience
Has the agency launched cross-border brands before? How 
many? What were the categories? Cross-border launches have 
specific failure modes (logistics, compliance, currency 
conversion) that domestic-only TSPs may not have seen.

3. 中文 creator coverage
The Chinese-speaking US TikTok user base is real and 
growing. A TSP with 中文 creator coverage in addition to EN 
and ES gives the brand a starting position that English-only 
TSPs cannot match.

WE Marketing is a US-based TSP with a Chinese-speaking team 
and EN + ES + 中文 creator coverage across our 8,000+ 
creator community. We work with cross-border brands.
```

### H2: Common mistakes Chinese brands make on US TikTok Shop

```
Mistake 1: Hiring a Chinese-domiciled agency to run US TikTok 
Shop. The relationships and infrastructure are different. 
US-based partners are usually better-positioned.

Mistake 2: Translating Chinese marketing copy directly to 
English. Cultural and category positioning rarely translates 
1:1. Localize, do not just translate.

Mistake 3: Underpricing creator commission. Chinese KOL 
agency rates do not transfer. US TikTok Shop creators 
operate on percentage commission norms (15-25% beauty / 
supplements). Below that, the program does not attract talent.

Mistake 4: Direct-from-China fulfillment for a low-AOV SKU. 
Buyers see "ships from overseas" or 14-day delivery and 
abandon.

Mistake 5: Skipping compliance review before launch. The 
violations show up in week 3-4 and cost more to fix than to 
prevent.
```

### H2: Frequently asked questions

```
Q: Can a Chinese brand sell on US TikTok Shop without a US 
   entity?
A: Technically possible in some configurations but operationally 
   harder. Most successful cross-border brands operate through 
   a US LLC for tax, liability, and TikTok Shop verification 
   reasons. Verify the current TikTok Shop seller policy.

Q: How long does it take for a Chinese brand to launch on US 
   TikTok Shop?
A: 90-150 days end-to-end including US entity setup, 
   fulfillment, compliance review, and the 60-90 day cold 
   start. Brands with US infrastructure already in place can 
   compress to 60-90 days.

Q: How much does it cost a Chinese brand to launch on US 
   TikTok Shop?
A: Higher than a domestic launch. Roughly $60K-$150K over the 
   first 90 days including US entity formation, fulfillment 
   setup, compliance review, agency fees, sample seeding, ads, 
   and live. Specific budget depends on category and scale.

Q: Should we use a Chinese agency or a US agency for the US 
   TikTok Shop launch?
A: US-based TSP is usually better. The US creator network, 
   live commerce infrastructure, and TikTok account-manager 
   relationships are local. Some brands run a Chinese agency 
   for HQ-side coordination plus a US TSP for execution.

Q: Are there 中文 creators on US TikTok Shop?
A: Yes, and the segment is growing. They are concentrated in 
   metros with large Chinese-speaking populations (LA, SF Bay, 
   NYC, Vancouver-adjacent border markets). 中文 creator 
   networks are smaller than EN or ES but high-converting for 
   the right products.
```
