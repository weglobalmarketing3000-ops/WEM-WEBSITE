## Article body

### Lead

```
"TikTok Shop or Amazon?" is one of the more common strategy 
questions we get from brand founders in 2026. The honest 
answer is "different platforms, different jobs, and the 
right answer depends on your category and stage."

The deeper answer is this: the platforms reward fundamentally 
different brand behaviors. A brand that wins on Amazon 
through search-led discovery and operational scale often 
underperforms on TikTok Shop in its first quarter. A brand 
built for TikTok Shop's creator-led discovery sometimes 
struggles to translate into Amazon's review-driven funnel.

This is a working comparison from our operator desk. We 
manage TikTok Shop programs, so we have a perspective bias. 
We have tried to call out where that bias matters.
```

### H2: How discovery works on each platform

```
Amazon: discovery is search-led. The buyer types a query, 
Amazon ranks listings by a mix of relevance, sales velocity, 
review count, and ad spend. The brand competes for keyword 
real estate.

TikTok Shop: discovery is creator-led and feed-based. The 
buyer is not searching. They are scrolling. A creator video 
catches them mid-feed, and the product link is a tap away. 
Per TikTok Business, short-form creator content drives the 
majority of TikTok Shop discovery.

Practical implication: Amazon rewards brands with strong SEO 
and operational depth. TikTok Shop rewards brands with strong 
creator partnerships and content velocity.
```

### H2: Conversion and AOV patterns

```
On Amazon, buyers are usually in-market. They came searching. 
Conversion rates on healthy listings often run 8-15% (Amazon 
public seller benchmarks vary; check Amazon Brand Analytics 
for current data).

On TikTok Shop, buyers are mid-feed and not in-market. The 
conversion math depends almost entirely on the creator video 
and live moment. Average order values tend to skew lower than 
Amazon for the same product because the impulse-buy mode is 
different.

Brands evaluating both should not expect Amazon-like 
conversion math on TikTok Shop, or vice versa.
```

### H2: Fee structure compared (directional)

```
Both platforms publish fee schedules. We are summarizing the 
shape, not specific numbers; verify the current fees for 
your category.

Amazon: referral fee (typically 8-15% of GMV depending on 
category) plus FBA fees if using Amazon's fulfillment 
(weight-based, location-based) plus optional ads spend.

TikTok Shop: platform commission (typically a flat percentage 
plus payment processing) plus the brand-set affiliate 
commission to creators (separate from platform fees) plus 
optional Spark Ads.

Practical implication: TikTok Shop's brand-side platform fees 
are often lower than Amazon's referral fees. But the affiliate 
commission to creators is an additional line that Amazon does 
not have in the same form. Net unit economics depend on which 
side you optimize.

Source: Amazon Seller Central fee schedule; TikTok Shop 
Seller Center / Academy fee schedule.
```

### H2: Side-by-side decision table

```
| Factor                    | Amazon                  | TikTok Shop              |
|---------------------------|-------------------------|--------------------------|
| Discovery mechanic        | Search                  | Creator content / feed   |
| Buyer state               | In-market               | Mid-feed, impulsive      |
| Time to first sale        | 30-45 days              | 7-14 days                |
| Primary brand lever       | SEO + reviews + ads     | Creator + content + live |
| Operational complexity    | Higher (FBA, taxes)     | Lower (catalog + creators)|
| Affiliate dependency      | Optional / low          | High / essential         |
| Best fit categories       | Search-driven products  | Demo-driven products     |
| Scale ceiling             | Very high               | Very high (still rising) |
```

### H2: When to start on each

```
Start on Amazon first if:
- Your category is search-driven (replacement parts, specific 
  technical products, repeat consumables with brand loyalty).
- You have strong reviews from prior channels you can move 
  over.
- Your team has operational depth on FBA, account health, 
  case management.

Start on TikTok Shop first if:
- Your product demos in 15 seconds (visible transformation, 
  problem-solution).
- You have access to creators or are willing to build a 
  creator program.
- AOV is in the impulse-buy band ($15-$60 for most categories).

Run both eventually if:
- You are scaling past $5M ARR and want diversified channel 
  exposure. Most brands at scale operate both.
```

### H2: Common mistakes when running both

```
Mistake 1: Identical content strategy on both. Amazon listing 
copy reads as marketing. TikTok creator videos read as 
authentic. Same content on both kills both.

Mistake 2: Same SKU pricing on both without thinking about 
the buyer mode. Amazon buyers compare prices across listings. 
TikTok Shop buyers compare against the next product in their 
feed. The price elasticity is different.

Mistake 3: Treating TikTok Shop as "an Amazon-like channel 
that needs more videos." It is structurally different commerce.
```

### H2: Frequently asked questions

```
Q: Should I launch on Amazon first or TikTok Shop first as a 
   new brand in 2026?
A: Depends on category. Demo-driven products with visible 
   transformation typically launch faster on TikTok Shop. 
   Search-driven products typically launch faster on Amazon. 
   For most consumer brands with a clear hero product, TikTok 
   Shop is the faster validation channel right now.

Q: Can I cross-promote between Amazon and TikTok Shop?
A: Yes, with caveats. TikTok Shop's affiliate program does 
   not natively link to Amazon listings, and TikTok's 
   policies on driving traffic off-platform have evolved. 
   Verify current platform rules before structuring 
   cross-promotion.

Q: Which platform has better unit economics?
A: Highly product-dependent. TikTok Shop's affiliate 
   commission is a real cost line. Amazon's FBA fees are a 
   real cost line. The breakeven calculation works differently 
   for each. Build the unit economics for your specific 
   product before assuming one is "cheaper."

Q: Do brands need a different team to run TikTok Shop vs 
   Amazon?
A: Most brands operating both have a clear functional split: 
   the Amazon team owns listings, ads, FBA, and account 
   health. The TikTok Shop team owns creator partnerships, 
   content, live, and shop ops. Skill overlap is limited.
```
