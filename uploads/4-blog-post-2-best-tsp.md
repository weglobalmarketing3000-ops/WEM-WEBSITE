### Section H2: What is a TikTok Shop Partner Agency?

```
A TikTok Shop Partner Agency, or TSP, is an agency officially 
certified by TikTok to manage the full TikTok Shop operation 
for a brand. That includes shop strategy, listing 
optimization, creator activation, affiliate program 
management, content production, live commerce, and ad 
management.

A TSP is not the same as:
• A general influencer agency (no shop operations expertise).
• A paid social agency (no creator network or live ops).
• A Shopify or DTC ecommerce agency (different platform model).

The TSP certification matters because TikTok gives certified 
agencies access to internal tools, beta programs, account 
manager support, and faster violation resolution that 
non-certified shops do not get.
```

### Section H2: 6 questions to ask before signing with any TSP

```
The questions that separate a working TSP relationship from a 
six-month write-off:

1. How many creators can you activate in week one?
   The answer should be 50+ for any US brand launch. Below 
   that, the agency is recruiting from scratch on your dime.

2. What does your live commerce infrastructure look like?
   Look for a host bench (3+ trained hosts), a live studio, 
   and at least 5 brand-owned live sessions per week as a 
   baseline.

3. What is your average creator commission rate?
   Below 15% on beauty or supplements suggests an agency 
   without negotiating leverage. Above 30% baseline suggests 
   they are giving away margin.

4. Do you handle compliance and violation resolution?
   "Yes" is the only acceptable answer. Brands without TSP 
   compliance support lose 5-15% of gross GMV to avoidable 
   violations.

5. What languages and markets do your creators cover?
   For US brands, EN + ES is mandatory. EN + ES + 中文 unlocks 
   the full US TikTok Shop market.

6. What is your reporting cadence and what metrics do you 
   report on?
   Daily dashboards on GMV, creator-level performance, and 
   ad ROAS are the floor. Weekly readouts and monthly 
   strategy reviews are standard.
```

### Section H2: Red flags to watch for

```
Five red flags that signal the wrong TSP:

1. They cannot name 5 brands they have launched in the past 
   12 months. Vague case studies are a sign of thin track 
   record.

2. Their creator network is "exclusive" but they cannot show 
   you a creator dashboard or roster. A real network is 
   queryable.

3. They charge a single flat monthly retainer with no 
   performance accountability. This rewards activity, not 
   results.

4. They cannot explain SPS performance, violation resolution, 
   or compliance workflows. Shop operations cannot be skipped.

5. They promise specific GMV numbers in pitch. Reputable TSPs 
   give ranges, not guarantees, because TikTok algorithm 
   variance is real.
```

### Section H2: When you don't need a TSP

```
Three cases where a TSP is not the right call:

• You have less than $200K annual revenue and are still in 
  product-market-fit testing. Hire a content creator and run 
  cheap tests yourself first.

• You are a $50M+ brand with an in-house creator team, live 
  studio, and shop operator. You may be better served by a 
  specialist consultancy than a full-service TSP.

• You sell B2B or considered-purchase products that need 
  long sales cycles. TikTok Shop is built for impulse and 
  demo-driven categories.
```
