## Candidate requirements

A candidate enters `qualified_source_backlog` only when:

- the complete first-party source is accessible;
- it contains a material operational question for U.S. brands, sellers, or creator teams;
- WEM can add a distinct decision framework, trade-off analysis, or operating system;
- it does not duplicate a live WEM article or an unfinished fixed-queue topic;
- current platform facts can be stated with safe source and account-level verification boundaries.

Store the source title, stable URL or ID, observed date, qualification decision, WEM angle, duplicate check, and discovery date.
