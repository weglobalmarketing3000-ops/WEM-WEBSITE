## Required cover grammar

- Warm-white editorial background with a pale-lavender field.
- Small blue category pill near the upper-left.
- Large high-contrast condensed title on the left.
- Short blue or dark subtitle below the title.
- Official pink WEM logo near the lower-left; use the real asset, never generated text.
- One cohesive blue-purple soft-3D operating scene on the right.
- A small number of topic-relevant objects connected as one scene.
- Generous whitespace and legibility at blog-card thumbnail size.

## Automatic rejection conditions

Reject and rebuild before deployment when any condition is true:

- The cover is a process diagram, dashboard, flowchart, or three-panel storyboard.
- The composition is full-bleed art without the established left title block.
- The image contains generated or malformed WEM lettering.
- Multiple internal background panels compete with the page background.
- Red, green, or other saturated blocks break the lavender/blue series palette.
- The title is crowded, clipped, weak at thumbnail size, or overlaps the scene.
- The scene is generic decoration that does not communicate the article's operating subject.
- The candidate has not been visually compared with all three approved references.

## Body visual boundary

Use two or three body visuals for the article's decision framework, comparison, workflow, or checklist. Localize visible text for Chinese. Body visuals may be diagrams; the cover may not.

## Pre-deploy visual readback

1. Open all three approved covers and the candidate at comparable scale.
2. Confirm the candidate reads as the next card in the same series without seeing its filename.
3. Inspect the optimized cover and thumbnail, not only the source PNG.
4. Render the blog index card, article desktop view, and 390 × 844 mobile view.
5. Stop before deployment if any automatic rejection condition remains.
