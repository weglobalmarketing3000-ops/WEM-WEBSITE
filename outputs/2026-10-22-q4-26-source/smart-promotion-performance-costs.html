How to View Program Performance and Costs?

09/24/2026

AI Summary

Smart Promotion now has a new Seller Center panel showing program performance, discount breakdown, exposure benefits, unlocked campaign access, actual fee rates, and actual costs. It affects sellers using or considering SP; check Seller Center → Marketing → Smart Promotion to review dashboard metrics, fee history, and order-level discount details. Actual charges appear in settlements as Smart Promotion Fee (3.5% of GMV) and, during mega campaigns, Smart Promotion Campaign Period Fee (1%); dashboard data updates on T+2.

Sellers can log in to Seller Center -> Marketing -> Smart Promotion to view the new panel (Page links: Local Sellers)

View Program Performance on the Data Dashboard

Page Overview
User Guide
Smart Promotion Value Summary section can help you measure your performance
  • GMV Contribution: This reflects how much of your GMV is driven by SP discounts. It shows the proportion of GMV generated from SP discounts relative to your total store GMV.
  • New Customers: This reflects how many new store customers were attracted by SP discounts. New customers here refer to those who placed their first order in your store within the last 365 days.
  • Orders: This reflects how many of your orders included SP discounts.
Platform discount breakdown by marketing tool can help you understand discount allocation
  • Coupon Discount Details: This shows the portion of orders with SP discounts that used SP-issued coupons. You can view the total coupon amount, the number of orders using coupons, and specific order details.
  • Product Direct Discount Details: This shows the portion of orders with SP discounts that received SP product incentive discounts. You can view the total product incentive discount amount, the number of orders with product incentive discounts, and specific order details.
  • Bonus Discount Details: This shows the portion of orders with SP discounts that involved the claiming or redemption of SP-issued bonuses. You can view the redeemed amount and the claimed amount. You can also view the total number of orders with redemptions, along with specific order details.
Note: Specific metric definitions can be viewed by clicking on the panel. You can also click the date module in the upper right corner of the panel to switch the displayed time range.

View Detailed Data Analytics

Page OverviewUser Guide
  1. View calculation formulas and historical trends for program value metrics
    1. You can click the Program Value module to open the side panel and dive into historical trends.
    2. Note: All data is displayed in your local time zone and is updated on a T+2 schedule.
  1. View order distribution by discount tool
    1. You can click "Discount Overview" in the discount module to open a side panel for order-level drill-down
    2. Orders are sorted by time from newest to oldest by default, and you can sort by order amount and discount amount
    3. When hovering over the discount amount of a single order, a toolbar appears explaining the breakdown of the discount amount by different tools
      1. For example: If an order receives a $10 total discount, the tooltip will show that the $10 consists of a $5 coupon, a $3 product direct discount, and a $2 bonus redemption.

View Actual Program Costs

Module
Illustration
Description
Orders
The "Smart Promotion" discount item on the order page represents the discount amount the consumer received on this order by using SP discount tools (e.g., coupons/product incentives).Please note: This does NOT represent your actual cost! It only represents the discount amount used by the consumer.
Settlements
You can see the actual cost of a single order on the settlement statement.The name of this fee is "Smart Promotion Fee", and the charging standard is: 3.5% of the order GMV.During campaign periods, you will see an additional fee charged. The name of this fee is "Smart Promotion Campaign Period Fee". This fee is only charged during mega campaigns, and the charging standard is: 1.0%-2.0% of the order GMV.
Settlement Report
You can check the fees related to Smart Promotion in the settlement details spreadsheet.How can I download the settlement report?To download the settlement report, please follow these steps:Go to Finances > Statements or Payouts in the Seller Center. Click Export. Select a date range to export. Your downloaded files can be viewed under Export History. Select Download.
How do I find Smart promotion fees in the report?
  • Step 1: Click the table named “report”.
  • Step 2: Check the Smart Promotion fees.
    • Note:
      • The Smart Promotion fee is charged during non-campaign periods, at a rate of 3.5% of your GMV.
      • The Smart Promotion campaign period fee is an additional Smart Promotion fee charged during major campaign events, at a rate of 1% of GMV.
You can use these two fee items to check your actual spending in the Smart Promotion program.
