What Is TikTok Shipping LIVE Flat Rate

09/21/2026

Applies to:United States
Order ManagementOrder Fulfillment PolicyManaging Orders & ShipmentLogistics Settings

AI Summary

Peak-season Standard Delivery rates will change for U.S. domestic orders from Oct 5, 2026 to Jan 17, 2027 PT, while CBT rates stay unchanged. For LIVE orders, packages under 5 lb use an automatic flat rate by weight tier, even for combined orders; over 5 lb they use weight- and distance-based pricing. To reduce cost, combine eligible LIVE orders from the same shopper and consider capped shipping fee. Estimated rates are available in the Shipping Calculator.

What Is TikTok Shipping LIVE Flat Rate - Current

A LIVE flat rate is applied to eligible packages weighing below 5 lbs(actual weight and dimensional weight), whether orders are shipped individually or combined.
Once effective, these special rates will automatically apply to packages created from orders placed during a LIVE session and no extra setting is needed.
For packages generated from LIVE orders:
  • Under 5 lb: The seller shipping rate is determined by the package weight tier, regardless of the package contains one order or multiple combined orders.
  • Over 5 lbs: The seller shipping rate is calculated based on both package weight and shipping distance. For more details regarding this rate card, please refer to <What is TikTok Shipping?>
The rate card below shows the current seller shipping rates for eligible packages created from orders placed during a LIVE session. TikTok Shop reserves the right to make a final determination on how the rate card information below applies to eligible packages.
Please note: this rate card applies to seller settlement. The shopper-side rate card will take effect independently, based on the seller’s shipping fee configuration.
To help manage your shipping costs, consider combining eligible orders placed by the same shopper during the same LIVE session. Combining multiple eligible orders into fewer packages may help reduce the total shipping charges.
You can also turn on Capped shipping fee for LIVE orders to limit the total shipping fee paid by a shopper during a LIVE session. Once the shopper reaches the configured cap, you will cover any remaining shopper shipping fees for eligible orders.

Cost Estimation

Use the Shipping Calculator to view estimated seller shipping rates:
  • For packages generated from LIVE orders and weighing <5 lbs, the calculator displays the applicable LIVE flat-rate card.
  • For packages generated from Non-LIVE, the calculator uses the standard weight- and distance-based rate card.
