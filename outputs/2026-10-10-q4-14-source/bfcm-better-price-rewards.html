[2026 BFCM] Better Price Rewards Registration Guide

09/29/2026

Applies to:United States
Product

AI Summary

BFCM now uses one unified registration flow: enter one product price and the system assigns the matching benefit tier automatically, including standard, Flash Sale/Premium Offers, or extra TikTok Shop subsidies. It applies to local, international, and eligible brand sellers. Register from Seller Center > Campaigns > Black Friday using online, bulk, or Excel upload; products register by SKU, different products can use different tiers, and inventory reservation is not supported.

How to Register: One Registration, Unlock Tiered Benefits

Registration Links:
Local Seller Registration Link🔗，International Registration Link🔗
During BFCM there will be a single unified campaign registration flow:
  • Enter one price, register once, and the system will automatically place products into the corresponding tier based on eligibility — no need to duplicate operations across multiple campaigns. (Eligible Brand Campaign sellers will register their product recruitment through this same entry and enjoy brand-exclusive benefits.)
  • Product-level registration: Sellers can register by product; sellers can choose different discount tiers for different products (by SKU), and benefits unlock according to the registered price. Inventory reservation is not supported.

Step 1: Go to the Campaigns Page and Start Registration

  • In Seller Center, go to the Campaigns page
    • Find the Black Friday campaign and click Register

Step 2: Review Campaign Details

  • On the campaign details page, review the campaign information and product price tiers. Each tier unlocks the corresponding Black Friday campaign benefits:
    • Register at the entry price: Unlock standard benefits
    • Register at a lower price: Unlock the Flash Sale & Premium Offers channel
    • Register at the lowest price: Unlock additional TikTok Shop subsidies
Note: Invited products from brand sellers may display additional benefit information

Step 3: Register Products

  • Click Register to open the registration page. You can register products in any of the following three ways; choose the option that best fits your needs.
    • Online registration
    • Online bulk registration
    • Upload file registration
3.1 Online Registration
  • Select this registration method and click it
  • Select the products you want to register
  • Enter product prices
  • Each product SKU displays multiple price tiers and their corresponding benefits
  • The best choice tier is selected by default. Sellers can choose another price tier as needed
  • You can also select Custom Price. A custom price must meet the campaign's price threshold
  • After submitting, review the information, preview it, and complete registration.
  • If SKU price tiers differ, the following message appears:Some SKUs qualify for different benefit tiers. The lowest tier will be applied across all SKUs.
3.2 Online Bulk Registration
  • Select the product range
  • Choose how to set prices:
    • Sellers can apply the platform's Recommended price to all selected products with one click. The benefits for that price will be displayed.
    • If you choose Percentage off to set prices in bulk, the benefits shown will correspond to the tier of each discounted price
  • Click Submit
3.3 Excel Bulk Upload
  • Select Pre-filled template to view the benefits for all three tiers
  • Click Generate template to download the template
  • Enter your desired prices in the template, then upload it

Step 4: Unlock More Benefits

  • On the Recommended Registration list, find products that can unlock additional benefits and click Register to enroll them
  • On the Registered Products Management list, review products eligible for higher-tier benefits and click Edit to change their prices
