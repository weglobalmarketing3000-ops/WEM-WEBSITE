AI-Generated Content Restrictions and Requirements for Product Listings Images

09/24/2026

Applies to:United States
Product Detail Policy
This requirement document explains how sellers should use AI-generated, rendered, or edited visuals in product listings. The goal is not to discourage AI-generated content (AIGC) entirely. The goal is to prevent misleading product displays where the product shown in videos or images does not match the actual product being sold.
For more information about content restrictions and requirements, refer to AI-Generated Content Restrictions and Requirements.

AIGC Product Image

AIGC is allowed when it accurately represents the real product. AI-generated images for products should never change product's size, color, shape, features, or performance, so that it no longer matches the real product. Using AI to fabricate, modify, or exaggerate product images in a way that no longer accurately represents the actual product being sold is considered misleading content.
This also includes using AI to change quantity or bundle contents, make an item appear larger or thicker, show unsupported accessories, show an incorrect installation position, make a static product appear animated, or show compatibility with vehicles, surfaces, parts, or environments not supported by the product.
For more information on image requirements for product listings, refer to Product Listing Policy.

Seller Requirements

Keep product specifications consistent

Sellers must ensure that AI-generated or edited visuals match the product page and the actual product the customer receives.
Do not use AI to:
  • Change the size, color, material, or shape of a product.
  • Show features or performance the product does not actually have.
  • Create scenes that make the product look more advanced, premium, functional, or different than it really is.
  • Display a product that conflicts with the product title, description, images, category, SKU, or customer delivery experience.

Do not turn 2D products into fake 3D products

Flat, acrylic, printed, sticker, poster, wall art, banner, decorative painting, and other 2D products must not be presented as raised, layered, embossed, relief, wood-grain, or three-dimensional objects unless the actual product is assembled or sold as a three-dimensional product.
This rule is especially relevant when the PDP title or image clearly indicates that the product is 2D, acrylic, printed, or flat, but the product images are not actual photos and use obvious 3D effects. Sellers should avoid AI visuals that use scene composition, perspective misalignment, edge processing, relief effects, embossing, stacked layers, 3D wood grain, or raised textures to hide the product’s flat or thin attributes.
Do not use AI to:
  • Turn flat products into fake 3D or rotating visuals.
  • Make thin printed products look thick, carved, layered, or embossed.
  • Use lighting, shadows, edges, or perspective to make a 2D item look like a physical 3D object.

Do not exaggerate size or scale

When the PDP contains product size information, AI-generated visuals must not distort the true physical dimensions of the product. Sellers should not use composition, perspective, labels, or reference objects to make the item look much larger, taller, thicker, or smaller than the stated size.
This requirement applies to all categories except fashion industry size-rule scenarios, where separate category-specific review standards may apply.
Do not use AI to:
  • Show a small item as if it is much larger than the stated size.
  • Place the product beside people, furniture, homes, vehicles, doors, or walls in a way that creates false scale.
  • Add labels or arrows that conflict with the actual product dimensions shown on the PDP.
  • Make an item look thicker, taller, wider, or heavier than the buyer will receive.

Do not substitute the product with a different main object

AI visuals must show the product being sold, not a different main subject. Sellers should not replace the sold product with animals, people, accessories, or lifestyle objects that cause customers to misunderstand what is included.
Examples include cat toy listings that use real kittens as the main subject, or robot puppy plush toy listings that use real dogs as the main subject. These images can mislead customers into thinking the listing is for a real animal or a different product instead of the toy.
Do not use AI to:
  • Replace a toy, plush, decoration, or accessory with a real animal or unrelated object.
  • Make the lifestyle scene more visually attractive by hiding or minimizing the actual product.
  • Show a product category that conflicts with the product title or listing category.

Keep SKU, bundle, and count information accurate

AI-assisted images must not make the listing appear to include different SKUs, bundles, quantities, or versions than the buyer will receive. Product images, SKU options, title, and description should all tell the same story about what is included.
Do not use AI to:
  • Use one listing for unrelated products or different product categories.
  • Show multiple products when the buyer receives only one or two pieces.
  • Show a bundle when items are sold separately.
  • Use one SKU image or claim for a different SKU variation.
  • Duplicate products in the image in a way that inflates the actual quantity.

Keep product attributes aligned with AI-assisted claims

Product attributes must match the claims made in AI-assisted images, video, title, description, and SKU-level content. If a listing contains multiple SKUs, sellers should check each SKU to ensure the image and claim match the selected variation.
Attributes that may create misleading claims include material, applicable devices, density, length, thickness, volume, specifications, and quantity. For example, a product description should not claim silk if the material attribute is cotton, and a product image should not imply a larger volume than the volume listed in the product attributes.
Do not use AI to:
  • Contradict material, volume, thickness, length, or quantity attributes.
  • Show device, vehicle, surface, part, or environment compatibility that is not supported by the product attributes.
  • Suggest included accessories or parts that are not listed in the SKU or product details.

Do not show fake or unsupported effects

AI visuals should not imply performance, effects, functions, motion, animation, or included items that the real product does not provide.
Do not use AI to:
  • Animate static products in unrealistic ways.
  • Add glowing, lighting, motion, or special-effect visuals that suggest unsupported functionality.
  • Show installation positions, usage environments, or performance results that the product cannot support.
  • Use rendered effects that hide the actual product appearance.

Misuse of AIGC Product Images

The platform may take appropriate enforcement actions against AI-generated content that violates the standards outlined above. Enforcement measures will vary based on the nature, severity, and frequency of violations.
