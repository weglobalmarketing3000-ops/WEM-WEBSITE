# WEM 30-Day TikTok Shop Policy-to-Operations Content Plan

**Decision date:** 2026-09-26 PT  
**Publication window:** 2026-09-27 through 2026-10-26 PT  
**Market:** United States  
**Publication model:** one new bilingual EN/ZH article per day through the existing Daniel daily publisher  
**Decision:** 30 topics passed the independent-search-question and duplicate-boundary gate. No topic exists only to fill the calendar.

## Editorial rule

This is not a 30-part policy summary. Each article must convert one current official rule or product update into one operator decision, one reusable tool, and one clearly bounded search answer. Official platform requirements must be labeled as official; WEM frameworks, examples, scorecards, checklists, and recommendations must be labeled as WEM interpretation. Volatile thresholds, dates, availability, fees, subsidies, and interface paths must be revalidated on the publication date.

The four-week sequence is intentional:

1. **Prepare the commercial system** — campaign eligibility, product truth, content control, pricing, and contract rights.
2. **Run pre-launch checks** — authorization, data access, discoverability, creator briefs, and promotional claims.
3. **Operate the event safely** — contribution margin, warehouse handoff, dispatch evidence, capacity, SLAs, returns, and automations.
4. **Recover and learn** — disputes, chargebacks, evidence, promotion economics, bundles, insurance, and cancellation controls.

“Active-event” and “post-event” articles are intentionally published before BFCM so operators can build the system before the official campaign window begins.
