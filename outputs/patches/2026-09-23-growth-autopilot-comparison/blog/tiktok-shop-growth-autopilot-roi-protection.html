TikTok Shop Growth Autopilot ROI Protection: What the 90% Threshold Covers

Wendy Lin · Sep 22, 2026 · 10 min read

Growth Autopilot's beta screen shows a useful promise, but it is easy to read too much into it. The screen we reviewed says that 90% of the ROI target is protected, says no conversions are required, and describes a separate growth-investment option. That does not turn an ad budget into a guaranteed profit plan. It gives a seller a threshold to verify against the account's actual terms and a set of numbers to reconcile after each seven-day cycle.

This article is about that accounting question: what is protected, what can still be charged, and what a seller needs to keep in the same ledger before approving a cycle. It is based on seller-provided beta screens reviewed September 22, 2026. Beta availability and terms can differ by account, market, and test cohort.

Start with the one calculation the screen supports

The example setup shows a 2.5 ROI target. Ninety percent of 2.5 is 2.25. That is the visible protection threshold. It is not a statement that the campaign will deliver 2.5 ROI, $7,500 in sales, a particular margin, or a cash refund.

The interface says eligible campaigns receive an ad credit. It does not show a complete credit formula, timing, caps, all exclusions, or how the credit interacts with tax, refunds, other incentives, or account status. Treat the ad credit as a line that must be confirmed after the cycle, not as money you can count before launch.

Campaign budget is not necessarily the total-cycle cost

The same screen separates a $3,000 campaign budget from a $300 growth investment. The visible example therefore has a $3,300 total setup amount before a seller evaluates any other business cost. The safe interpretation is not that every account will be charged exactly $300. It is that a seller should never judge the cycle from the campaign-budget field alone when a separately charged growth-investment feature is enabled.

Here is a deliberately hypothetical example. If the beta report displayed $7,500 of attributed gross revenue and the account actually charged the full $3,000 campaign amount plus $300 growth investment, $7,500 divided by $3,300 is approximately 2.27. That is a total-cycle-spend calculation. It is not evidence of incrementality, profit, or an entitlement to a credit. If the account did not charge the full investment, the denominator changes. If returns, discounts, affiliate commission, fulfillment, or product cost are excluded, the number is still not contribution.

Keep three questions separate

The first question is the beta protection question: did the cycle meet the account-specific protection conditions and threshold? The second is the billing question: what campaign spend and additional growth investment were actually charged? The third is the commercial question: after product cost, fulfillment, commission, discounts, returns reserve, and media, did the business retain acceptable contribution?

Those questions can have different answers. A cycle may qualify for a credit while still producing weak contribution after all costs. A cycle may show an attractive dashboard ROI while a separately charged growth investment changes the total-cost view. A profitable cycle may not qualify for a credit because a visible condition was not met. Do not collapse those outcomes into one headline number.

Build a cycle-reconciliation table before launch

Use one owner and one ledger for each seven-day cycle.

Ledger field What to record Why it matters
Campaign spend actually charged Final billed amount, not only the selected budget Delivery may differ from a setup field
Growth investment actually charged Separate billed amount and its account description It can change total-cycle media cost
Attributed gross revenue The beta or campaign reporting value and its definition Useful for controlling the campaign, not proof of incremental profit
Confirmed ad credit Amount, status, timing and the terms used Credit is not a cash refund or a promise before confirmation
Contribution after other business costs Revenue less product, fulfillment, discount, commission, returns reserve and media This is the business decision number

Before the cycle starts, capture screenshots of the budget, target, protection wording, investment selection, schedule and restrictions. Name the campaign owner, inventory stop, contribution floor and pause authority. The beta screen says changes are constrained after a cycle begins, so the right time to settle the ledger logic is before the start time, not after an unexpected charge appears.

Do not copy standard GMV Max protection rules into this beta

TikTok's public documentation for standard GMV Max ROI protection is useful background, but it is not a substitute for the Growth Autopilot screen. Standard documentation describes daily calculations, daily order requirements and exclusions. The beta screen we reviewed explicitly says no conversions are required and frames the program in seven-day cycles. Those are different visible operating contexts.

Use the public GMV Max material to understand how protection programs can have eligibility conditions. Use the beta account's own terms to decide what applies to this cycle. If a seller cannot see the full terms, the honest decision is to treat the credit as unconfirmed rather than filling in the missing formula from a forum post or a different campaign type.

A practical cycle close

At the close of a cycle, export or screenshot the final campaign report, billing view and protection status. Confirm actual rather than planned spend. Tie the revenue number to the report definition. Record the credit only after its status and amount are visible. Then compare total-cycle media cost with contribution after other business costs.

The smallest useful next action is simple: add a separate "growth investment actually charged" column to the same working sheet that holds campaign cost and contribution. That one column stops a common reporting mistake before it becomes a budget decision.

For the broader beta setup and seven-day-cycle context, read the Growth Autopilot beta guide. For a readiness gate before any automated product campaign, see When Should a Brand Turn On GMV Max?.

Frequently asked questions

Does 90% ROI protection mean the campaign is guaranteed to hit my target?

No. The visible beta screen supports a protection threshold, not a guarantee of target ROI, sales, profit, or a cash refund.

What is 90% of a 2.5 ROI target?

It is 2.25. That calculation does not reveal the full credit formula or every eligibility condition.

Is growth investment part of the campaign budget?

The beta screen presents it separately. Reconcile the amount actually charged alongside campaign spend before judging total-cycle cost.

Is an ad credit the same as profit?

No. Confirm the credit first, then calculate contribution after product, fulfillment, commission, discounts, returns reserve and media.

Can I use the public GMV Max daily-protection rules for Growth Autopilot?

No. They are useful background, but the beta screen has its own visible seven-day and no-conversions context. Verify the account-specific terms.

Growth Autopilot 的 90% ROI 保障：额外增长投入后，这笔账怎么算？

Wendy Lin · 2026 年 9 月 22 日 · 10 分钟阅读

Growth Autopilot beta 页面里的“90% ROI 保障”很容易被看成一个稳赚承诺。我们在 2026 年 9 月 22 日核对的页面写着：ROI 目标的 90% 受保障，并注明“无需转化”；同一处还出现了单独的增长投入选项。更稳妥的理解不是“预算有保底利润”，而是：卖家要把保障门槛、实际扣费和真实贡献拆开核对。

这篇只回答钱该怎么算，不重复介绍整个 beta 功能。页面证据来自卖家提供的 beta 截图；开放范围、条款和金额都可能因账户、市场和测试批次而不同。

先算截图明确支持的一个数字

截图示例的 ROI 目标是 2.5，90% 就是 2.25。这代表页面所写的保障门槛，不代表广告一定跑到 2.5，也不代表保证销售额、保证利润或现金退款。

页面写的是符合条件可获得广告金 credit，但没有公开完整的计算公式、到账时间、上限、全部排除条件，也没有说明它与退款、税费或其他激励怎么叠加。因此，广告金应当在周期结束、状态和金额都确认后才记入账，而不是在开广告时先当作收入。

广告预算不一定等于这七天的总成本

同一截图把 3,000 美元广告预算和 300 美元增长投入分开显示。这个示例的设置总额是 3,300 美元。它不表示每个账户都会扣 300 美元；它提醒卖家，开启单独投入时，不能只看 campaign budget 一栏。

举一个明确标注为假设的例子：如果平台归因的总收入显示为 7,500 美元，账户实际扣了 3,000 美元广告费和 300 美元增长投入，那么 7,500 ÷ 3,300 约等于 2.27。这是按总周期媒体成本计算的数字，不是增量证明，不是利润，也不等于一定能拿到广告金。实际扣费不同，分母就不同；若未扣掉货品成本、履约、佣金、折扣和退货准备金，它更不能代表贡献利润。

三个问题一定要分开

第一是保障问题：这个周期是否满足本账户条款和门槛？第二是扣费问题：实际扣了多少广告费和单独增长投入？第三是经营问题：扣除货品、履约、佣金、折扣、退货准备金和媒体成本后，是否还有可接受的贡献？

三者可能同时给出不同答案。某个周期可能符合广告金条件，但扣完所有成本后仍然不赚钱；dashboard ROI 看起来很好，但单独增长投入改变了总成本；生意上有贡献，也可能因为未满足某个条款而没有 credit。不要用一个“ROI 很好”把这三件事合并。

每个周期都用同一张对账表

项目 记录什么 为什么
实际广告扣费 最终账单金额，不只看设置预算 实际 delivery 可能不同
实际增长投入 单独扣费金额与账户页面说明 它会改变总周期成本
平台归因总收入 beta 报表数字与口径 可用于管理广告，不等于增量利润
已确认广告金 金额、状态、到账时间和对应条款 不是预先承诺的现金退款
扣除其他成本后的贡献 收入减货品、履约、佣金、折扣、退货准备金和媒体 才是经营决策数字

周期开始前，保存预算、ROI 目标、保障说明、增长投入、排期和限制的截图；写明负责人、库存停止线、贡献底线和暂停权限。beta 页面写明周期开始后变更受到限制，所以账怎么记要在开始前定好，不要等出现意外扣费后再猜。

不要把常规 GMV Max 的保障条款直接套过来

TikTok 对常规 GMV Max 的公开保障文档可以作为背景，但不能代替 Growth Autopilot 的 beta 条款。常规文档谈按天计算、每日订单门槛和排除条件；我们看到的 beta 页面明确写着“无需转化”，并按七天周期展示。可见的经营语境不同。

公开文档能提醒我们：保障计划会有资格条件。真正决定这一个周期的，仍是你账户内显示的 beta 条款。看不到完整条款时，正确做法不是从论坛或别的广告类型补出公式，而是把 credit 记为“未确认”。

周期结束时怎么关账

导出或保存最终广告报表、账单视图和保障状态。先确认实际而不是计划扣费；再记录归因收入的口径；广告金在金额和状态可见后才入账；最后用总媒体成本计算扣完其他成本后的贡献。

最小但有效的下一步：在现有广告成本与贡献表中，增加一列“实际增长投入扣费”。这能避免把设置预算误当总周期成本。

想了解 beta 的七天周期背景，请看 Growth Autopilot beta 指南。投放前的经营准备，可参考 品牌什么时候适合开 GMV Max？。

常见问题

90% ROI 保障是否代表一定达到目标 ROI？

不代表。它是可见页面里的保障门槛，不是目标 ROI、销售额、利润或现金退款保证。

ROI 目标 2.5 的 90% 是多少？

是 2.25。这个计算不能推导出完整广告金公式或所有资格条件。

增长投入属于广告预算吗？

beta 页面把它单独列出。核算总周期成本时，应该和实际广告扣费一起记录。

广告金等于利润吗？

不等于。先确认 credit，再扣除货品、履约、佣金、折扣、退货准备金和媒体成本。

可以把常规 GMV Max 的日保障规则直接用于 Growth Autopilot 吗？

不可以。公开规则是背景；Growth Autopilot beta 要以本账户显示的七天周期和具体条款为准。
