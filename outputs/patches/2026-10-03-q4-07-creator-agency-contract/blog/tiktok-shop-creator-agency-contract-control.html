TikTok Shop Does Not Become a Party to Your Creator or Agency Contract

Wendy Lin · Oct 3, 2026 · 15 min read

Direct answer: the platform connection does not replace your contract

Direct answer: TikTok Shop may help a seller discover or work with a creator, agency or other service provider, but the current U.S. Seller Terms say the seller is responsible for the agreement and TT Commerce & Global Services is not a party to it. The terms also say TikTok does not act as the seller's representative and is not responsible for enforcing those third-party contracts.

That boundary changes the operating job. A profile, marketplace listing, Partner Center connection, chat, commission setting or platform workflow is not a substitute for a contract that names the actual parties, work, acceptance rules and exit duties. Treat platform access as a route to a commercial relationship, not as the commercial control itself.

What the current source actually says

Section 5.1 says sellers are solely responsible for contracts and terms made with creators or other third parties, including parties found through Seller Center or TikTok Shop Partner Center. It states that TT Commerce & Global Services is not a party to those agreements, does not act as the seller's representative, and does not determine the amount or terms of commissions or other service payments.

Section 5.2 adds that TikTok has no liability for claims relating to those contracts or the third party's services and will not enforce the agreement. Appendix 2 describes Partner Services as services contracted directly between the seller and the applicable Partner. It says the service contract is solely between seller and Partner and that TikTok itself does not provide Partner Services.

Those are source facts. The checklists below are WEM operating guidance. They do not decide enforceability, local law, employment classification, tax, intellectual property or privacy questions. Route those matters to qualified counsel.

Split one relationship into six controlled records

Parties and authority: name the legal entities, signers and approved channels. Confirm who may change budget, grant access, approve content or bind the business.

Scope and exclusions: list channels, markets, products, campaigns and services included. State what is excluded. A vague promise to “manage TikTok Shop” is not an operational scope.

Deliverables and acceptance: define file types, quantities, deadlines, review rounds, claim evidence, approval owner and what counts as accepted. Separate delivery from acceptance.

Commercial terms: record fees, commission, reimbursable costs, invoice timing, payment triggers and any change-control process. A dashboard value does not settle every off-platform commercial term.

Rights and evidence: name usage rights, channels, term, territory, edits, paid amplification, data access, retention and proof of approval. Do not infer rights from payment.

Exit and survival: define notice, final delivery, credential removal, data return or deletion, outstanding payment, content takedown, license expiry and the evidence that closes each duty.

Use an owner matrix instead of one overloaded manager

The brand owner approves business scope and spend. The creator or agency owner confirms capacity, delivery and subcontractors. The operator controls accounts, product facts, deadlines and readbacks. Qualified legal reviewers handle enforceability, rights, privacy, classification, indemnities and jurisdiction. One creator manager should not be forced to invent missing legal or product facts.

Record one decision owner and one evidence owner for every material term. The decision owner authorizes the result. The evidence owner preserves the signed version, approval, delivery and readback. When those roles are unnamed, teams often discover after a missed deadline that everyone assumed someone else owned the contract.

Build a release gate around each deliverable

Before work starts, confirm the signed version, active scope and required access. Before content or campaign launch, compare the deliverable with the brief, product truth, claim evidence, price and approved rights. At release, save who approved what version and where it may run. After release, confirm the live URL, account, spend and date. At close, remove access and reconcile files, data, invoices and surviving rights.

A platform status can support this process, but it cannot answer every contractual question. “Connected,” “accepted,” “commission set,” or “service completed” may describe platform state. They do not automatically prove that the brand received every promised file, obtained a reusable license, approved additional spend or completed offboarding.

Operational example: a creator agency manages a launch

Consider a hypothetical U.S. launch in which an agency recruits ten creators and coordinates content. The contract should identify whether the agency only coordinates or also contracts with creators, who pays creators, who approves claims, whether the brand may reuse videos, who owns raw files, and which accounts the agency may access. The brief should link each deliverable to a SKU and deadline. The release ledger should record the final asset, approval and live URL.

If two creators miss the deadline, the team should use the agreed replacement or change process. It should not assume TikTok will compel delivery. If the agency relationship ends, the exit checklist should remove account access, return brand data, identify content that can remain live, document licenses that survive, and reconcile unpaid amounts. This is not a legal conclusion; it is a way to make the operating facts visible before counsel resolves disputed terms.

The smallest useful next action

Open one active creator or agency agreement today. Create four columns: promise, owner, evidence and exit condition. Put the highest-risk missing item at the top and assign a due time. Do not approve the next deliverable until its scope, acceptance rule and evidence location are clear. If the missing item is legal, privacy, tax, employment or intellectual-property language, pause the operating decision and route it to qualified counsel.

TikTok Shop 不会替你承担达人或 Agency 合同：签约前要写清什么

Wendy Lin · 2026 年 10 月 3 日 · 15 分钟阅读

直接答案：平台连接不等于平台替你承担合同

直接答案：TikTok Shop 可以帮助卖家找到达人、Agency 或其他服务商，但当前美国站 Seller Terms 明确，卖家自己对与这些第三方签订的协议负责，TT Commerce & Global Services 不是协议一方。条款还说明，TikTok 不是卖家的代理人，也不负责执行这些第三方合同。

因此，主页信息、Service Market 展示、Partner Center 连接、聊天记录、佣金设置或平台工作流，都不能代替一份写清真实签约方、工作范围、验收规则和退出责任的合同。平台提供的是商业关系入口，不是合同控制本身。

当前官方来源到底说明了什么

Section 5.1 说明，卖家对与达人或其他第三方制定的合同及条款负责，包括通过 Seller Center 或 TikTok Shop Partner Center 找到的合作方。TT Commerce & Global Services 不是这些协议的一方，不代表卖家，也不决定佣金或其他服务费用的金额和条款。

Section 5.2 进一步说明，TikTok 不承担与这些合同或第三方服务有关的索赔责任，也不负责执行卖家与达人或其他第三方之间的合同。Appendix 2 将 Partner Services 定义为卖家与具体 Partner 直接签订的服务，服务合同只存在于卖家和 Partner 之间，TikTok 本身不提供 Partner Services。

以上是来源事实。下面的清单属于 WEM 运营建议，不判断合同可执行性、当地法律、劳动关系、税务、知识产权或隐私问题。这些内容必须交给有资格的专业人员。

把一个合作关系拆成六份可控记录

签约方与权限：写清法律主体、签字人和批准渠道，确认谁能修改预算、开放账号、批准内容或代表公司作出承诺。

范围与排除项：列出包含的渠道、市场、商品、活动和服务，也写清不包含什么。“负责 TikTok Shop”不是可以执行的范围。

交付与验收：定义文件格式、数量、截止时间、修改轮次、Claim 证据、批准负责人以及什么状态才算验收。交付不等于验收。

商业条款：记录服务费、佣金、可报销成本、账单时间、付款触发条件和变更流程。平台看板里的一个数字不能自动覆盖所有站外商业约定。

权利与证据：写清使用权、渠道、期限、地区、修改、付费放大、数据访问、保存期限和批准证据。不能因为已经付款就推定已经获得权利。

退出与继续有效条款：定义通知、最终交付、权限移除、数据返还或删除、未结费用、内容下架、授权到期，以及每项责任关闭时要保留的证据。

用负责人矩阵，避免一个 Creator Manager 扛所有问题

品牌负责人决定商业范围和预算。达人或 Agency 负责人确认产能、交付和分包。运营负责人控制账号、商品事实、时间和上线回读。法律专业人员审核可执行性、权利、隐私、劳动关系、赔偿和司法辖区。Creator Manager 不应该替其他部门制造缺失事实。

每个重要条款至少写一个决策负责人和一个证据负责人。决策负责人授权结果，证据负责人保存签署版本、批准记录、交付物和上线回读。如果这两个角色没有名字，延期之后往往会发现每个人都以为别人负责合同。

给每个交付物设置发布 Gate

开工前确认已签版本、有效范围和必要权限。内容或活动上线前，对照 Brief、商品事实、Claim 证据、价格和已批准使用权。发布时保存批准人、最终版本和允许使用的位置。上线后回读 URL、账号、花费和日期。合作关闭时，移除权限，并核对文件、数据、账单和继续有效的权利。

平台状态可以支持流程，但不能回答所有合同问题。Connected、Accepted、Commission Set 或 Service Completed 可能只描述平台状态，并不自动证明品牌已经收到全部文件、获得可复用授权、批准额外花费或完成权限回收。

运营示例：Creator Agency 管理一次新品发布

假设一个美国站新品项目由 Agency 招募十位达人并协调内容。合同要写清 Agency 只是协调还是也与达人签约、谁给达人付款、谁批准 Claim、品牌能否复用视频、原始文件归谁，以及 Agency 可以访问哪些账号。Brief 把每项交付物连接到 SKU 和截止时间，发布台账保存最终素材、批准记录和上线 URL。

如果两位达人延期，团队应该使用合同中的替换或变更流程，不能假设 TikTok 会强制对方交付。合作结束时，退出清单要移除账号权限、返还品牌数据、确认哪些内容可以继续上线、记录继续有效的授权并结清费用。这不是法律结论，而是在专业人员处理争议条款前把运营事实做清楚。

今天最小的下一步

今天打开一份正在执行的达人或 Agency 合同，建立四列：承诺、负责人、证据、退出条件。把风险最高的缺失项放到第一行并指定完成时间。在范围、验收规则和证据位置明确之前，不批准下一项交付。如果缺失的是法律、隐私、税务、劳动关系或知识产权语言，暂停运营决定并交给合格专业人员。

常见问题

在 Seller Center 找到达人后，TikTok Shop 会成为合同方吗？

不会。当前 Seller Terms 明确，卖家对与达人或第三方的协议负责，TikTok 不是协议一方。

TikTok Shop 会替品牌执行达人或 Agency 合同吗？

当前条款说明，TikTok 不负责执行卖家与达人或其他第三方之间的合同。

达人合同至少要写什么？

至少写清范围、交付物、期限、审批、费用、使用权、证据、变更、终止和退出后的责任。

在 Partner Center 选 Agency 等于平台担保服务吗？

不等于。Appendix 2 将 Partner Services 定义为卖家与 Partner 直接签约的服务，并非 TikTok Shop 自身提供的服务。

谁来审核法律条款？

涉及司法辖区和法律风险的内容应由合格律师审核。本文是运营控制框架，不是法律意见。

今天最小的动作是什么？

打开一份正在执行的合同，指定负责人，并在批准下一项交付前核对范围、验收、证据和退出条款。
