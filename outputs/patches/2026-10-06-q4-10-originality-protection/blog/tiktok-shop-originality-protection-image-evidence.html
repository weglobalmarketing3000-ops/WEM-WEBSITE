TIKTOK SHOP U.S. · ORIGINALITY PROTECTION

TikTok Shop Originality Protection: Build the Image Evidence Before Copycats Appear

WE Marketing Team · Oct 6, 2026 · 15 min read

Direct answer: TikTok Shop's Originality Protection program can protect qualifying original main product images for eligible sellers, but the platform record should not be your first or only proof. Build an image provenance ledger before publication. Keep the source file, creation record, ownership or authorization documents, approved final version, first-publication evidence and protection readback connected to the same asset ID.

The current official program page explains automatic verification, registration and listing-admission interception. It also says the Proactive Push and Quick Removal Risk Alert feature will be available shortly. That means a responsible operator must separate capabilities documented as current from a capability announced for later availability. The operating system below does not promise eligibility, automatic protection or enforcement outcome.

Read the current program boundary first

The Originality Protection program is available on an eligibility basis. Selected sellers may be enrolled automatically, and other sellers may be nominated by their TikTok Shop account manager. Participating sellers need an Intellectual Property Protection Center account. Once enrolled, the platform evaluates main product images during normal listing and automatically registers qualifying images as protected assets in IPPC under the corresponding product ID.

Not every uploaded image qualifies. The current criteria require a main product image, first publication of that image on TikTok Shop and sufficient clarity. Images already present on an earlier listing are not eligible. The official FAQ says secondary images, detail shots and videos are outside this program's coverage. Each protected image remains valid for one year from registration.

The program page says failed image verification does not block the seller's own listing. The product can still be listed and sold, but the image is not added to the protected bank, and that verification decision is not appealable. This distinction matters: listing status, OP registration and underlying ownership evidence are three different states.

Operating rule: protection status is a platform result; provenance is the brand's evidence of how the image was created and what rights it holds.

Build the ledger before the first upload

Create one asset record for every final main image. Give it a stable internal ID. Record the SKU, product ID when available, photographer or designer, shoot date, source-file location, filename, file hash, edit history, approval owner, rights document, intended territory, permitted channels and planned first-publication surface.

Store the original capture or design file, not only the compressed marketplace export. Preserve useful metadata where lawful and practical. Keep contact sheets, shot lists, briefs, invoices, approval records and version history. A polished JPEG on a shared drive may show the final image, but it does not by itself explain who created it, when, under what agreement or whether the brand received commercial rights.

For internal teams, connect the creator to the employment or assignment record that governs the work. For freelancers, studios and agencies, confirm the contract addresses ownership or license scope, edits, territory, term, sublicensing and marketplace use. For supplier-provided images, do not assume permission travels with the product. Obtain written authorization or a license that matches the seller entity and intended use.

Control first publication as an operating event

The current program uses first publication on TikTok Shop as an eligibility criterion. Therefore, the listing team should not treat image upload order as a casual production detail. Before launch, identify the approved seller account, product, main-image order, date and operator. Freeze the final file and checksum it. Record where any earlier marketplace or test publication occurred.

If the same image has already appeared on an earlier TikTok Shop listing, do not rewrite history or create a misleading record. Mark the image as not eligible under the first-publication criterion and decide whether to create a genuinely new original asset. Do not make superficial edits merely to imitate originality. The ledger should reflect the real creation and publication sequence.

At release, retain a screenshot or exported record showing the listing, image and timestamp, plus the internal approval. After the listing is processed, check the IPPC protected-assets record or the weekly Seller Center summary described in the source. Save the readback date and result. Uploaded, listed, verified and protected are separate stages.

Use an evidence packet, not a folder of loose files

Packet sectionWhat it should proveTypical evidence
Asset identityThe exact image in questionInternal ID, source file, approved export, hash, SKU and product ID
Creation provenanceWho created it and whenRaw file, shoot log, brief, edit history, contact sheet and invoice

Keep the packet readable and bounded. Do not include unrelated personal information, credentials or confidential material that is not needed. If the matter becomes a legal dispute, have qualified counsel decide what to preserve, disclose or submit.

Separate four controls that teams often collapse

Original creation: the brand can trace how the image was made. Rights: the seller owns the image or has permission appropriate to the use. OP eligibility: the seller and image meet the current program criteria. Enforcement: the platform detects a protected-image use and applies the available process. Success in one control does not automatically prove the others.

A brand may own an image that is not eligible for OP because it was not first-published on TikTok Shop or is not a qualifying main image. A seller may have written authorization to use another party's protected image, but still need that document if a listing is recalled. A protected asset record helps, but the brand should retain its creation and rights evidence for business continuity and dispute handling.

Do not present Risk Alert as a current universal workflow

The official page describes Proactive Push and Quick Removal through IPPC Risk Alert, but explicitly says the feature will be available shortly. Treat that statement as a future-availability boundary. Do not train teams to expect an alert, advertise one-click removal as universally live, or measure response SLAs around a screen the current account may not have.

What the team can do now is keep the IPPC account ready, review protected assets, retain listing-admission and enforcement notices actually received, and maintain the evidence packet. If Risk Alert later appears in the current account, verify the interface, scope and permissions before adding it to the SOP. A product announcement is not an operational readback.

Prepare both sides of a possible enforcement event

If another seller appears to use the brand's image, capture the listing URL, seller, product, image, date and comparison without altering the original evidence. Match the suspected image to the internal asset ID and IPPC protected record. Use the official complaint path available to the account. Do not contact or accuse a seller publicly based only on visual similarity; route uncertain ownership or infringement questions to qualified intellectual-property counsel.

If your own listing is recalled for using a protected image, open the violation notice and use the appeal reason that matches the facts. The current page distinguishes ownership or authorization from written authorization by a rights holder. Supporting documents should be clear and readable, match the seller account, cover the United States and remain within valid dates. Do not fabricate, backdate or stretch a license beyond its terms.

The source says a seller with a legitimate basis may appeal a recalled listing. That appeal is different from an OP verification decision, which the page says is not appealable. Route the record to the correct process instead of treating every negative result as the same dispute.

Operational example: a studio creates launch images

Consider a hypothetical U.S. seller hiring a studio for a new SKU. Before the shoot, the brand assigns asset IDs and attaches the brief, product references and contract. The agreement states the applicable rights and delivery obligations. After the shoot, the operator stores raw files, contact sheets, final edits and approvals, hashes the approved exports and records the photographer, date and SKU.

The listing owner publishes the selected main image through the approved seller account and records the live listing and time. After processing, the IP owner checks IPPC and the weekly summary, then records whether the image was protected. If it was not protected, the listing can still remain live; the team records the result rather than claiming coverage. If the same image later appears elsewhere, the team can assemble one coherent packet instead of reconstructing history under deadline.

The smallest useful next action

Choose the next SKU scheduled for photography. Create one row with six fields: asset ID, source file, creator, rights document, first-publication plan and protection readback. Assign an owner for each missing field before the main image is uploaded. This single row is more useful than waiting for a copied listing and then searching through chat, drives and invoices.

Source notes and execution boundary

This original WE Marketing operating framework draws on the current TikTok Shop U.S. Originality Protection Program, revalidated October 6, 2026. The source covers eligibility-based enrollment, IPPC participation, automatic verification and registration of qualifying main product images, first-publication and clarity criteria, one-year protection, listing admission interception, enforcement and appeal evidence. It states that Proactive Push and Quick Removal through Risk Alert will be available shortly; this article does not present that feature as currently live. Program access, interfaces and decisions may change. Verify the current account and use qualified intellectual-property counsel for ownership, licensing, infringement or legal-preservation decisions.

Common questions

Does every TikTok Shop product image receive Originality Protection?

No. The current source limits automatic protection to eligible participants and qualifying main product images that are first-published on TikTok Shop and meet clarity requirements.

Do I need a registered trademark or copyright certificate?

The program page says a registered trademark or copyright certificate is not required for OP coverage, but brands should still preserve ownership and authorization evidence.

Are secondary images, detail shots and videos protected by OP?

The current FAQ says only qualifying main product images are covered. Secondary images, detail shots and videos are not covered by this program.

What happens if my image fails verification?

The listing can still be published and sold. The image is simply not added to the protected bank, and the verification decision is not appealable.

Is the IPPC Risk Alert available now?

The current official page says the Proactive Push and Quick Removal Risk Alert feature will be available shortly. Do not plan operations as if it is already live for every account.

What evidence should an appeal packet include?

Use the reason that matches the facts and provide clear ownership proof, rights-transfer documents, or written authorization showing the parties, permitted use, United States coverage and valid term.

TikTok Shop 原创保护：被抄图之前，先把图片证据链建好

WE Marketing Team · 2026 年 10 月 6 日 · 15 分钟阅读

直接答案：TikTok Shop 的 Originality Protection 可以为符合条件的卖家保护合格原创商品主图，但平台记录不应该是品牌第一份、也不应该是唯一一份证明。图片发布前就要建立来源台账，把源文件、创作过程、所有权或授权文件、批准版本、首次发布时间和平台保护回读连接到同一个 Asset ID。

当前官方页面介绍了自动验证、注册和上架拦截，也明确写明 Proactive Push 与 Quick Removal 的 Risk Alert 功能将于近期提供。因此，运营必须区分已经写明可用的能力和预告中的能力。下面的系统不承诺卖家一定入选、图片一定被保护，或平台一定作出某种执法结果。

先读清当前项目边界

Originality Protection 目前按资格开放。部分卖家会被自动纳入，其他卖家可以由 TikTok Shop Account Manager 提名。参与者需要 Intellectual Property Protection Center，也就是 IPPC 账号。卖家入选后，平台会在正常上架过程中检查商品主图，并把符合条件的图片自动登记为 IPPC 中对应 Product ID 下的受保护资产。

不是所有上传图片都符合条件。当前标准要求是商品主图、该图片首次发布在 TikTok Shop，并达到清晰度要求。已经出现在更早商品 Listing 上的图片不符合首次发布条件。官方 FAQ 说明，辅图、细节图和视频不在本项目覆盖范围内。每张受保护图片从登记日起有效一年。

项目页面也说明，图片验证失败不会阻止卖家自己的商品上架。商品仍能正常销售，只是图片不会进入受保护图片库，而且这一验证决定不能申诉。商品已上架、OP 已登记和品牌拥有权利，是三个不同状态。

运营规则：保护状态是平台结果；来源证据是品牌用来说明图片如何产生、自己拥有什么权利的记录。

第一次上传前就建立来源台账

为每张最终商品主图建立一个资产记录，并分配稳定的内部 ID。记录 SKU、可用时的 Product ID、摄影师或设计师、拍摄日期、源文件位置、文件名、文件 Hash、修改历史、批准负责人、权利文件、使用地区、允许渠道和计划首次发布位置。

保存原始拍摄或设计文件，而不只是 Marketplace 使用的压缩图。在合法且实际可行的范围内保留元数据，同时保存 Contact Sheet、Shot List、Brief、发票、批准记录和版本历史。共享盘里的一张精美 JPEG 只能说明最终画面，无法单独说明是谁、在什么时间、按照哪份协议创作，也无法证明品牌是否获得商业使用权。

内部团队创作的图片，要连接适用的雇佣或权利归属记录。Freelancer、Studio 和 Agency 创作的图片，要确认合同写清所有权或授权范围、修改、地区、期限、再授权和 Marketplace 使用。供应商提供的图片不能因为品牌采购了商品就默认获得权限，应取得与卖家主体和计划用途相匹配的书面授权或 License。

把首次发布当成一个受控运营事件

当前项目把 TikTok Shop 首次发布作为图片资格标准之一。因此，上架团队不能把图片上传顺序当成无关紧要的制作细节。上线前要确认批准的 Seller Account、商品、主图顺序、日期和执行人，冻结最终文件并计算 Hash，同时记录该图片是否曾在其他 Marketplace 或测试 Listing 中发布。

如果同一图片已经出现在更早的 TikTok Shop Listing 上，就不要修改历史或制造误导记录。把图片标记为不符合首次发布条件，再决定是否需要制作真正的新原创资产。不要通过轻微改色或裁剪伪装原创。台账必须反映真实创作和发布时间线。

放行时保存能够显示 Listing、图片和时间的截图或导出记录，并连接内部批准。平台处理后，再到 IPPC 的受保护资产记录或来源描述的 Seller Center 周报进行回读，保存回读日期和结果。已上传、已上架、已验证和已保护是四个不同阶段。

建立证据包，不要只留一堆散文件

证据包部分需要证明什么常见证据
资产身份争议的是哪一张准确图片内部 ID、源文件、批准版本、Hash、SKU 与 Product ID
创作来源谁在什么时间创作Raw File、拍摄记录、Brief、修改历史、Contact Sheet 与发票
权利链卖家为什么可以使用和保护雇佣记录、权利转让、License、承包协议或授权书
发布历史图片何时在何处首次出现放行记录、Listing URL、时间和归档截图
平台状态TikTok Shop 实际登记或执行了什么IPPC 资产回读、周报、通知、投诉或申诉结果

证据包要清晰且范围有限，不要加入无关个人信息、账号密码或不需要的机密材料。如果问题进入法律争议，由合格律师决定应该保存、披露或提交什么。

把团队最容易混在一起的四个控制拆开

原创创作：品牌能够追溯图片如何产生。权利：卖家拥有图片，或持有与用途相匹配的授权。OP 资格：卖家和图片满足当前项目标准。执法：平台识别受保护图片的使用，并执行当前可用流程。一个控制通过，不会自动证明其他控制。

品牌可能真正拥有图片，但因为不是 TikTok Shop 首次发布，或不是合格商品主图，所以没有 OP 资格。卖家可能获得了使用他人受保护图片的书面授权，但 Listing 被召回时仍要提交该文件。受保护资产记录很有用，但品牌仍要保留创作与权利证据，以支持业务连续性和争议处理。

不要把 Risk Alert 写成当前通用流程

官方页面描述了 IPPC Risk Alert 的 Proactive Push 与 Quick Removal，但明确写明该功能将于近期提供。这是一个未来可用性的边界。不要培训团队一定会收到提醒，不要宣传所有账号都能一键移除，也不要围绕当前账号可能根本没有的页面制定响应 SLA。

团队现在能做的是准备好 IPPC 账号，检查受保护资产，保存实际收到的上架拦截或执法通知，并维护证据包。如果以后 Risk Alert 出现在当前账号里，再核验界面、范围和权限，然后加入 SOP。产品预告不是运营回读。

同时准备可能发生的两类执法事件

如果发现其他卖家疑似使用品牌图片，先保存 Listing URL、卖家、商品、图片、日期和对比，不要修改原始证据。把疑似图片匹配到内部 Asset ID 和 IPPC 受保护记录，并使用当前账号真正提供的官方投诉路径。仅凭画面相似，不要公开指责对方；所有权或侵权事实不确定时，交给合格知识产权律师。

如果自己的 Listing 因使用受保护图片被召回，就打开违规通知，并按照事实选择申诉理由。当前页面区分“自己拥有或获得授权”和“权利人提供书面授权”。文件要清晰可读、与 Seller Account 匹配、覆盖美国，并且仍在有效期内。不能伪造、倒签，或把 License 扩大到原条款之外。

来源说明，有合法依据的卖家可以对被召回 Listing 提交申诉。这与 OP 图片验证决定不同；后者按当前页面说明不能申诉。要把记录送到正确流程，而不是把所有负面结果当成同一类争议。

运营场景：Studio 为新品拍摄主图

假设一个美国站卖家为新 SKU 聘请 Studio。拍摄前，品牌分配 Asset ID，并连接 Brief、商品参考和合同。协议写清适用权利和交付要求。拍摄后，运营保存 Raw File、Contact Sheet、最终版本和批准记录，为批准导出文件计算 Hash，并记录摄影师、日期和 SKU。

Listing Owner 通过批准的 Seller Account 发布主图，记录上线 Listing 和时间。平台处理后，IP Owner 检查 IPPC 和周报，并记录图片是否获得保护。如果没有获得保护，商品仍可以保持上线；团队只记录真实结果，不宣称已经覆盖。如果以后出现同图，团队可以快速形成一份连贯证据包，而不是在截止时间下翻找聊天、云盘和发票。

现在就做的最小动作

选择下一款准备拍摄的 SKU，先建立一行六字段记录：Asset ID、源文件、创作者、权利文件、首次发布计划、保护回读。主图上传前，为每个缺失字段指定负责人。这一行记录，比等到图片被复制后才到处寻找历史更有价值。

来源说明与执行边界

这套 WE Marketing 原创运营框架基于 2026 年 10 月 6 日重新核验的 TikTok Shop U.S. Originality Protection Program。来源涵盖按资格入选、IPPC 参与、符合条件商品主图的自动验证与登记、首次发布与清晰度标准、一年保护期、上架拦截、执法和申诉证据。来源写明，通过 Risk Alert 主动推送并快速移除的能力将于近期提供；本文没有把它描述为当前已经上线。项目资格、界面和决定可能变化。执行前核验当前账号，所有权、License、侵权和法律保全问题应交给合格知识产权律师。

常见问题

是不是所有 TikTok Shop 商品图都会获得原创保护？

不是。当前来源把自动保护限制在符合资格的参与者，以及首次发布在 TikTok Shop、达到清晰度要求的合格商品主图。

一定要有注册商标或版权证书吗？

项目页面说明，获得 OP 保护不要求注册商标或版权证书，但品牌仍应保留归属和授权证据。

辅图、细节图和视频会被 OP 保护吗？

当前 FAQ 说明，只有符合条件的商品主图在该项目覆盖范围内，辅图、细节图和视频不在本项目保护范围。

图片验证失败会影响商品上架吗？

不会。商品仍可正常上架和销售，只是该图片不会进入受保护图片库，而且这一验证决定不能申诉。

IPPC Risk Alert 现在已经上线了吗？

当前官方页面写明 Proactive Push 与 Quick Removal 的 Risk Alert 功能将于近期提供。不要把它当成所有账号已经上线的能力。

申诉证据包应该放什么？

按事实选择申诉理由，并提交清晰的所有权证明、权利转让文件，或写明双方、允许用途、美国地区范围和有效期限的书面授权。
