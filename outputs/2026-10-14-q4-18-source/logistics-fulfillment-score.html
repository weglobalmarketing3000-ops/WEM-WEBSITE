[New] SPS: Introduction to Logistics and Fulfillment Score

09/21/2026

Applies to:United States
Shop ExperienceUnited States

AI Summary

The SPS Logistics & Fulfillment Score enters preview-only trial run from 2026/9/21–10/14 and launches 2026/10/15; current benefits still use the old version during trial. The biggest change is a shift from on-time delivery to faster delivery, splitting timeliness into Dispatch Time and Transit Time, while adding Free Shipping Coverage as a +0.5 bonus. Only eligible sellers can preview it, rollout is phased, and appeals are unavailable during trial.

New Version Trial-Run Instruction

SPS new version will start the trial run phase from 2026/9/21-10/14, and is scheduled to officially launch on 2026/10/15.
  • During the trial run, the new version of the score is for preview only and will not affect merchants’ current benefits. Scores and benefits will still be based on the existing version.
  • The new version preview will be rolled out gradually in batches. Different sellers may see the entry at different times. Please refer to the timeline below. If you do not see the entry yet, please follow future platform notifications and page updates.
  • Due to special considerations, sellers whose shop calculation category is Pre-owned will be included in the second-week rollout and will not be opened in the first week.
  • Only sellers who meet the Shop Performance Score eligibility threshold can access the new version preview page.

What Is the Logistics & Fulfillment Score?

Logistics & Fulfillment Score is a scoring system that measures fulfillment and logistics experience, with a maximum of 5 points. Together with the two dimensions of Product Satisfaction Score and Customer Service, it forms the Shop Performance Score.
For dimension aggregation, please go to: Shop Performance Score calculation formula.
❗️Key change: from “delivered on time” to “delivered faster,” with greater emphasis on fulfillment service experience.
  • Timeliness: broken down into clearer, more attributable, and more improvable stages — Dispatch Time and Transit Time;
  • Fulfillment service: Seller Fault Cancellation Rate is retained, and Free Shipping Coverage is added as a bonus metric.
Metric types:
  • Scoring metric: included in weight allocation
  • Bonus metric: add points directly when standards are met

How to Calculate Logistics & Fulfillment Score?

Calculation Formula

Logistics & Fulfillment Score = Dispatch Time Score × weight + Transit Time Score × weight + Seller Fault Cancellation Rate Score × weight + Free Shipping Coverage bonus
Free Shipping Coverage is a bonus metric and is not included in weight allocation. When Free Shipping Coverage reaches the bonus threshold, an additional 0.5 points will be added to the dimension score, capped at a maximum of 5 points.

#Step 1: Metric Calculation

[Calculation Principles]
  • A seller's performance is only compared with shops under the same calculation category.
  • Shop calculation category: determined by whether a category accounts for ≥60% of the seller’s order share or GMV share over the past 90 days.
  • Oversize items category split: Given their higher logistics and fulfillment complexity, sellers primarily selling oversize items are placed in a separate calculation category based on the logistics-side oversize definition.

[New] Free Shipping Coverage (Bonus metric)

  • Free Shipping Coverage = Delivered orders with $0 paid shipping fee / Total delivered orders in the past 7 days
    • Default bonus threshold: When Free Shipping Coverage ≥ 80% → +0.5 to the dimension score, capped at 5.
    • Special bonus threshold: For categories with lower AOV and greater difficulty in offering free shipping, the threshold is lowered to 60%. When Free Shipping Coverage ≥ 60% → +0.5 to the dimension score, capped at 5.
      • Applies to the following 7 categories: Collectibles, Non-natural Crystal, Non-sports Trading Cards, Sports Trading Cards, Food & Beverages, Fashion Accessories, Trading Cards & Accessories.

[No Change] Seller Fault Cancellation Rate

  • Seller Fault Cancellation Rate (SFCR) = Number of orders canceled due to seller's fault / Number of all orders in the To Ship - Awaiting Shipment stage in that period
    • Includes cancellations handled by the platform and 4PL;
    • Orders related to FBT are excluded from the numerator but included in the denominator.
For more details, please go to: Seller-Fault Cancellation Rate Requirements

#Step 2: Convert Metrics into Scores

  • Metrics are scored from 0 to 5, with standards set for 1–4 points.
    • Metrics are converted based on the score standards within the same shop calculation category.
    • Meeting a score threshold earns at least that score; better performance earns a higher score.
Example
Shop A and B both have Kids' Fashion as their calculation category. For the Kids' Fashion, the 3-point standard for Dispatch Time is 48 hours, and the 4-point standard is 40 hours. Shop A’s and Shop B’s dispatch times over the past 30 days are 40 hours and 41 hours, respectively.
  • Shop A’s dispatch time score is exactly 4 points, while Shop B’s dispatch time score falls within the 3–4 point range.
  • Based on Shop B’s dispatch time ranking within the Kids' Fashion category, Shop B’s dispatch time score is calculated as 3.9 points.
*Data is for illustration only and does not reflect actual scoring standards.

#Step 3: Derive the Final Score

  • Different metrics impact consumer experience differently, so Logistics & Fulfillment metric scores are weighted when aggregated.
  • Weights vary by the seller’s main category.
  • Free Shipping Coverage is a bonus item: it does not receive weight. When Free Shipping Coverage reaches the bonus threshold, an additional 0.5 points will be added to the dimension score, capped at a maximum of 5 points.

[Special Scenarios]

Scenario 1: Less-Than-Truckload (LTL) Orders
  • Excluded from both Dispatch Time and Transit Time calculations, including numerator and denominator.
Scenario 2: Cross Border Direct Shipping Orders
  • Excluded from Transit Time calculations, including numerator and denominator.
Special scenario calculation
Example: If a seller delivered 10 orders in the past 30 days and 4 are LTL orders, only the remaining 6 orders are used to calculate average Dispatch Time and Transit Time.

How to Appeal?

  • New Version:
    • During the new-version trial run: New Version scores are for preview only; related scores and benefits do not take effect, so appeals are not supported yet.
    • After the official launch: Appeals will open gradually after launch, expected in October. If you dispute an order’s calculation, submit an appeal via the appeal entry.
  • Old Version: All appeal functions in the current version operate normally. For specific appeal methods, please go to: How to Appeal Your Shop Performance Score (SPS) Metrics

FAQ

Why does my Logistics & Fulfillment page look different from other sellers' ?

The fulfillment module is being upgraded and rolled out in phases, so sellers may see different page versions.
Please rest assured, this does not affect scoring: all sellers follow the same Fulfillment score calculation rules and standards. No action is required; please stay tuned for follow-up platform notifications.

How are oversize item categories split?

Oversize Item definition: Items weighing over 50 lbs or with any side longer than 26 feet, excluding specified L1 categories.
Items hitting the following L1 categories should not be considered oversize: Fashion Accessories, Modest Fashion, Kids' Fashion, Shoes, Menswear & Underwear, Pre-Owned, Collectibles, Womenswear & Underwear, Jewelry Accessories & Derivatives, Virtual Products, Books, Magazines & Audio, Food & Beverages.
If a seller mainly sells oversize items, they are assessed in a separate oversize items category.
V7 includes 9 oversize items categories in total, subject to volume requirements:
  • L1: Furniture
  • L2: Indoor Furniture, Outdoor Furniture
  • L3: Mattresses, Sofas, Scooters Equipment, Cycling, Ride-on Toys, Power Supplies

If a shop uses multiple logistics services, how are Dispatch Time or Transit Time calculated?

Example uses Dispatch Time; Transit Time follows the same logic.
In the past 30 days, the seller delivered two orders:
  • Order A: FBT, actual dispatch time 18h. Dispatch Time corresponding to the 5-point standard for the seller’s calculation category is 15h. Thus, dispatch time for order A is 15h.
  • Order B: Seller Shipping (3PL), actual dispatch time 10h, calculated normally.
Shop Dispatch Time = (15h + 10h) / 2 = 12.5h
*Data is for illustration only and does not represent actual conditions.

How are combined or split packages calculated?

All Logistics and Fulfillment metrics are calculated by order, not by package. One order split into multiple packages counts as one order; multiple orders shipped in one package count as multiple orders.
