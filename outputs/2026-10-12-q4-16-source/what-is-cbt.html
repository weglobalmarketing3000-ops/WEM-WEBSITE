What is Collection by TikTok (CBT)

10/08/2026

Applies to:United States

AI Summary

Collection by TikTok (CBT) lets eligible US sellers keep their current warehouses while using TikTok pickup plus nationwide last-mile shipping, with free pickup in selected states, rates from $2.99, and fulfillment-policy protections if orders are dispatched on time. To join, register in Seller Center > Logistics > Shipping if your warehouse is in an eligible area or averages 20+ daily packages where pickup isn’t yet supported, and make sure parcels stay within 50 lb and size limits; registration is offline Nov 7, 2026–Jan 5, 2027.

What is Collection by TikTok (CBT)?

Collection by TikTok (CBT) is a combination of pickup + last-mile services provided by TikTok Shipping. Pickup is currently available in selected key areas across the United States. Eligible sellers can purchase last-mile shipping labels through TikTok Shop and access pickup services and exclusive logistics benefits without changing their existing warehouses.

What Are the Key Benefits of CBT?

  • Free pickup service: Access free pickup service in Southern California, New York, New Jersey, Massachusetts, Pennsylvania, Illinois, Georgia, Texas and Florida
  • Lower Shipping Costs: Enjoy TikTok Shop’s exclusive negotiated rates! CBT rates start from $2.99! There is also a dedicated flat rate for LIVE orders, where shipping rate is determined by the package weight tier only.
  • Limited-time Promotion: If your daily package volume exceeds 200 packages, you can receive a $0.50 discount per package for shipments in Zones 1–4, on top of the existing CBT logistics service rate card.
  • Fulfill from Your Current Warehouses: You can continue using their existing warehouses and fulfillment workflows, and access CBT services without making any changes to their warehouse setup.
  • Improve Fulfillment Compliance Policy: Enjoy exclusive exemptions from penalties related to On-Time Delivery Rate (OTDR) and Negative Review Rate (NRR) if you dispatch your orders on time. Additionally, any delivery delays or logistics-related negative reviews (excluding “out of stock/wrong item” and “damaged items in package”) caused by TikTok Shop logistics will not impact your Seller Performance Score (SPS), making fulfillment much easier.
  • VTR Exemption : Benefit from a special exemption on Valid Tracking Rate (VTR), freeing you from worries about uploading tracking information
  • Better Claim Process: TikTok will compensate for lost, damaged, or undelivered packages if caused by logistics, making compensation faster and hassle-free.
  • Optimized Carrier Recommendations: TikTok Shop automatically selects the best carrier based on package size, weight, and destination, eliminating the need for manual selection.
  • Reliable Carrier Network: Primarily powered by USPS, covering nationwide delivery, ensuring peace of mind with every shipment.

How to join CBT

Please note:
  • To ensure a smooth experience for CBT during the peak season, the CBT registration tool will be offline from November 7, 2026, to January 5, 2027.
  • If you would like to join CBT services, please complete your registration before the deadline.

Eligibility Requirements

To join CBT service, you need to meet the following requirements:
  1. Your chosen warehouse is located within a CBT eligible zip code. See list for eligible zip codes in Southern California, New York, New Jersey, Massachusetts, Pennsylvania, Illinois, Georgia, Texas and Florida
  2. If your warehouses are not supported by CBT pickup services yet, you can register for CBT services when your average daily package volume of warehouse address has been greater than 20 packages within the past 30 days.
  3. If your warehouses are currently supported by CBT pickup services, there's no minimum daily package requirement to join CBT.
    • If your warehouse address currently supported by CBT is located in selected areas in Georgia, Illinois, Florida, or certain regions of Texas, your CBT enrollment will take effect immediately after registration with no review period.
  4. Your product meets the weight and dimension requirements below:
    • Weight Limit: ≤ 50 lbs
    • Dimension Limit: Longest side ≤ 60 cm / 23.62 inches; Sum of all three sides ≤ 150 cm / 59.06 inches
  5. Your chosen warehouse supports adding CBT as a shipping carrier (Just like adding USPS or Fedex)
  6. Your chosen warehouse is able to sort CBT orders and hand them over to the CBT service carrier. For more details, please refer to <CBT Order Processing: Warehouse Operations SOP>
Please note: CBT service is available for warehouse addresses maintaining an average daily volume of 20 or more packages. Warehouses under addresses below this threshold will be reassigned to TikTok Shipping - Standard Delivery.

How to register

If you meet the eligibility requirements above, you can register online by going to Seller Center > Logistics > Shipping page. For detailed instructions, please refer to <How to Sign Up for CBT>

Shipping Governance and Charges

To sustain logistics service quality and enhance the platform's fulfillment experience, TikTok Shipping will impose fees on sellers for certain non‑compliant shipping practices, as detailed below:

Dimension Surcharge

  • Applicable sellers: TikTok Shipping - Standard Delivery and Collection by TikTok (CBT)
  • Effective date: 10/05/2026
  • Fee structure: A dimension surcharge will apply if a package meets any of the following criteria:
    • Longest side exceeds 48 inches, or package volume exceeds 10,368 cubic inches: $10.15 per package
    • Longest side exceeds 96 inches, or package volume exceeds 17,280 cubic inches: $79.99 per package

Weight Surcharge

  • Applicable sellers: TikTok Shipping - Standard Delivery and Collection by TikTok (CBT)
  • Effective date: 10/05/2026
  • Fee structure: A weight surcharge will apply if a package meets any of the following criteria:
    • Actual weight exceeds 50 lb: $10.15 per package
    • Actual weight exceeds 100 lb: $79.99 per package

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

If you want to know more about TikTok Shipping or other FAQ, please refer to <What is TikTok Shipping>

Service Subscription

  1. CBT vs. Standard Delivery— What’s the difference?
  • Standard Delivery serves everywhere: TikTok Shop provides shipping labels, then you sort orders and drop off or third-party courier gets picked up.
  • CBT provides pick-up service only in Southern California, New York, New Jersey, Massachusetts, Pennsylvania, Illinois, Georgia, Texas and Florida. Warehouses need to sort orders, hold them separately, and wait for TikTok Shop to collect them. Plus, you’ll get better rates.
  1. Can I use CBT to inbound inventory to an FBT warehouse?
No, CBT can only be used for pickup and shipping of customer orders.
  1. Can CBT orders only be delivered to designated areas, or can they be delivered nationwide?
CBT pickup service is only available in designated areas, but its shipping service supports delivery across the United States.
  1. What's the weight/dimension limit to using CBT
CBT service is subject to weight and dimension limits. Please refer to the requirements below to avoid delivery disruptions. If the shipment exceeds the following weight or dimensional limits, please contact your AM for further assistance.
Collection by TikTok (CBT)
  • Weight Limit: ≤ 50 lbs
  • Dimension Limit: Longest side ≤ 60 cm / 23.62 inches; Sum of all three sides ≤ 150 cm / 59.06 inches

Billing

  1. How are shipping fees settled?
TikTok deducts shipping fees from either your GMV or deposit. Billing happens after successful delivery & transaction completion. Sometimes you might see a second charge adjustment after the courier’s actual weight audit.
  • Please refer to Billing FAQ
  1. How are shipping fees calculated based on order weight and dimensions?
Shipping fees for TikTok Shipping are calculated based on the greater of the product’s volumetric weight (also known as dimensional weight) and actual weight, as maintained by the seller. Sellers are required to ensure that product dimensions and weight are accurate.
How to calculate volumetric weight:
When shipping via Seller Center, the estimated shipping fee shown during label creation is calculated using the product dimensions and weight maintained in Seller Center.
When shipping via an ERP system, the estimated shipping fee is calculated based on the product dimensions and weight maintained in the ERP.
After the parcel is handed over, the logistics service provider will measure the actual weight and dimensions of the shipment and may adjust the shipping fee accordingly. The platform reserves the right to recalculate and adjust the final shipping charges based on the verified actual weight and dimensions of the shipped items.
For example: You have a CBT package that is 16” x 12” x 10” and weighs 5 lbs. The volumetric weight: 16*12*10/166 ≈ 12 lbs. Since the volumetric weight (12lbs) is heavier than the actual weight (5lbs), the shipping fee will be calculated based on volumetric weight (12lbs).
